Latest Announcements:
- Registration will take place September 5, 2026. Returning students please arrive at 3pm. New students, please arrive at 2pm. It is NOT first come first serve. Please do not rush. Being early does not guarantee a spot. 
- Parents/Guardians must fill out a registration form (available at the door)
- Returning students will have reserved spots. All new students will undergo an interview and be placed into a lottery system to be drawn at random after registration day. Parents will be notified during the week after registration of either acceptance or waitlist. 
- School fees are $400 for the year. Returning students shall pay in full on registration day. New students only make payment after acceptance.
- New students must bring a copy of their report card or ID from their regular school to verify their age and grade level. 
- Returning students show their Able report card to verify previous enrolment. 
- All results for placement and class lists will be available on the first day of classes (2026 Sept 12)
